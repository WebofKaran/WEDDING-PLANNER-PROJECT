TYPING ANIMATION

Add these attributes to any plain-text paragraph:
<p data-typewriter data-type-speed="16">Your long paragraph here.</p>

The about paragraph in index.html and home.html already uses it.
Keep site.css and site.js included on the page.
16 means roughly 16 milliseconds per visible letter. Smaller values type faster; larger values type slower. Total duration is capped at 8 seconds for long paragraphs.

HOW IT WORKS
IntersectionObserver starts the animation when at least 25% of the paragraph enters view. JavaScript splits words into character spans and reveals them using requestAnimationFrame. Hidden letters still reserve their space so the paragraph keeps its width, wrapping and height. A small rose cursor follows the active letter. Once finished, ordinary text is restored. It runs once per page load and resets on refresh.
Screen readers receive the full paragraph immediately. Reduced-motion users see the full text without animation. Paragraphs containing links or other nested markup are left intact.

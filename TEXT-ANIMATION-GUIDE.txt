TEXT ANIMATION — ELAN WEDDINGS

The logo animation now replays each time index.html or home.html loads or refreshes.
All page animations wait for the intro animationend event. The subtitle, headline words, description and buttons then enter in sequence, using delays relative to intro completion. Photo, scroll-reveal and typing observers also wait for this shared completion signal.
Visitors who prefer reduced motion see content immediately.

HOW TO ANIMATE YOUR OWN TEXT
Add class="text-reveal" to a text element:
<h2 class="text-reveal">Your beautiful heading</h2>

Add a delay to stagger another line:
<p class="text-reveal" style="--text-delay: .2s">Your supporting text.</p>

WORD-BY-WORD HEADLINE
<h2 aria-label="Your beautiful day">
  <span class="text-word" style="--word:0" aria-hidden="true">Your</span>
  <span class="text-word" style="--word:1" aria-hidden="true">beautiful</span>
  <span class="text-word" style="--word:2" aria-hidden="true">day</span>
</h2>
Keep aria-label equal to the full heading for screen readers.

SETTINGS IN site.css
--text-delay: controls when animation begins.
110ms: delay between headline words.
1s / 1.1s: animation duration.
translateY(22px): upward travel distance.
blur(4px): initial softness, fading to clear text.

The text-reveal entrance runs on page load. Use the existing class="reveal" on a separate section for scroll-triggered section entrances.
Home hero delays are configured separately to follow the logo animation.
No libraries or frameworks are needed.
